Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Easy Table: Smart Web Data Extractor. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how our browser extension operates in the v1.2.x series.

2. Zero Data Collection Policy (Local-First)

Easy Table remains a "Local-First" application.

  • No Background Monitoring: We do not track your browsing history or monitor your keyboard.
  • Local Processing: Core features like extraction, editing, and styling happen entirely inside your browser.
  • No Mandatory Accounts: No login is required for basic features.

3. Use of Permissions

We only request permissions essential for table extraction and editing:

  • clipboardRead Accessed only when you manually click "Import from Clipboard" to load Markdown/JSON data.
  • activeTab Used to detect and highlight tables on the active page during user interaction.
  • storage Used to save your styled table data, preference settings, and extraction history locally.
  • identity Used for optional Google Sheets synchronization via OAuth2 (only if triggered by user).

4. Optional Third-Party Sharing

Data only leaves your device when you explicitly use sharing features:

  • Google Sheets: Data is sent to Google's servers only when you choose to sync.
  • Public Sharing: Encrypted data is temporarily stored on our secure cloud if you generate a "Public Share Link".
