Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Easy Table: Smart Web Data Extractor. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how our browser extension operates in version 2.0.0.

2. Local Processing

Table extraction, editing, comparison, combination, and export are performed in your browser. Table data and settings used by the extension are stored locally in your browser. The extension does not require an account.

3. Use of Permissions

Version 2.0 requests the following permissions:

  • activeTabHelps the extension interact with the active page to detect and highlight tables.
  • storageStores tables and preferences locally in the browser.
  • unlimitedStorageAllows the extension to store larger amounts of table data locally.
  • clipboardReadReads clipboard content when you choose to import from the clipboard.

The extension runs a content script on web pages to detect tables and provide the floating toolbar. Browser-restricted pages may not permit extension scripts.

4. Exported Data

When you export a file or copy data, the result is saved or placed on your clipboard through your browser. What you do with that data afterward is up to you.
